## Package preparation

1. In Partner Center create a new `MSIX or PWA app` and reserve `??PDF`.
2. Open Product identity and copy the exact values for `Package/Identity/Name`, `Package/Identity/Publisher`, and publisher display name.
3. Rebuild the folder distribution from the Store-aware source.
4. Run `build_store_msix.ps1` with the exact Partner Center identity values.
5. Run the Windows App Certification Kit against the final package/build.
6. Upload the final MSIX in Partner Center.
7. Complete properties, age ratings, Store listing, pricing/availability, and certification notes.
8. Submit for certification.

## Important

Do not submit `build/modern-shell/EoingPDF.Shell.unsigned.msix`; that file is the sparse identity shell-integration experiment. The Store submission artifact must be the full `EoingPDF-{VERSION}-Store-x64.msix` produced by `build_store_msix.ps1` after the Partner Center identity is known.
